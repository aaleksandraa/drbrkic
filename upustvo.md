You are working on the real website of:

**ZU Dr Brkić — zdravstvena ustanova, Doboj, Bosnia and Herzegovina**

Your task is to **design and implement a completely refined homepage experience** based on the existing project, supplied project documentation, official logo, real photograph of the clinic, existing content, backend data and current technical architecture.

This is not a generic website generation task.

I want you to act as:

* senior digital art director;

* senior frontend engineer;
